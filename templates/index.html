Statutory Framework

Four-Tier Institutional Governance Architecture

How citizen-reported problems systematically advance through scientific validation, academic incubation, industrial capitalization, and departmental procurement.

TIER 01 Intake & AI

Grassroots Problem Dossier Intake

Citizens submit structured community challenges with geotagged media. Assisted Intelligence triages domain, identifies duplicates, and estimates severity.

Lead: District Nodal Desks & AI Engine
TIER 02 Verification

Government Departmental Gazette

Nodal officers verify ground veracity, assess departmental budget alignments, and sanction verified issues as Official State Technological Challenges.

Lead: Relevant State Ministries (DWSD, Agr, Mines)
TIER 03 Prototyping

HEI Prototyping & Peer Review

Scholars, faculty, and research labs (BIT Mesra, IIT-ISM, NIT) formulate engineering solutions, conduct bench testing, and obtain IP clearance.

Lead: Higher Education Institutions & R&D Cells
TIER 04 Procurement

CSR Capitalization & State Deployment

Industrial partners sanction milestone grants. Validated TRL-7 solutions receive single-window procurement clearance for statewide deployment.

Lead: Industry Partners & State Procurement Boards
Verified Output Metrics

Audited Societal Impact & State Milestones

Quantifiable outcomes delivered across public health, agrarian stability, and technical capacity through institutional collaboration.
